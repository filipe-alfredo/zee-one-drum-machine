ZeeOne Drum Machine — Help & Shortcuts

• Keyboard Shortcuts: Press Space to Play/Stop playback. Press C to clear active steps on the current pattern.

• Clear Kit vs Clear All: Clear Kit hides all drum rows on the right panel so you can build a minimal kit from scratch. Clear All erases the active notes on the current pattern.

• Pattern Groups & Workspace: Collapse/expand or hide entire pattern groups in the left panel. Click Save to export your entire Workspace (groups, patterns, visible tracks, BPMs, and timing offsets) to a .json file.

• Timing Offset (-50ms to +50ms): Use Kit Offset or each track's individual millisecond box to anticipate (-ms) or delay (+ms) hits in real time.
